Introduction

Find answers to common questions about payments, cards, and account security.

We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard), Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal. You can add multiple payment methods in Your Card.

Card payments are usually processed within a few seconds. In some cases, your bank may take 1–3 business days to reflect the charge on your statement.

Go to Profile → Payment Account → Your Card. Tap "Add New Card" to add a card. To remove a card, open Your Card and use the manage option next to the card.
